OCT 14 UPDATE

Thoughts on the New Record

Been locked in the studio for the last three weeks. The energy here is different. We stripped back a lot of the heavy production we leaned on for the last album. Everything feels more raw, more intentional.

Yesterday, we recorded this acoustic progression that just came out of nowhere. No click track, just playing to the room. It has this haunting quality that I can't seem to shake. I think it's going to be the opening track.

I want this record to feel like you're sitting in the room with us. No polish, just the truth. Thanks to everyone who's been supporting the process. Your messages keep us going when the 3 AM burnout hits.

More updates soon. Might drop a rough demo of that acoustic track later this week if we can get the mix right. We're experimenting with some analog synths for the bridge section.
